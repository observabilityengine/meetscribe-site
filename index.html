How it works

Three ways in, one library, about five minutes the first time.

  1. Record it, or bring what you have

    Record from your microphone inside MeetScribe, or drop in a recording: MP3, WAV, M4A, MP4, FLAC, WebM. It transcribes offline, on your machine. Already have transcripts? Bring the exports from Fathom, Otter, Fireflies or Zoom, in TXT, VTT, SRT or JSON.

  2. It all lands in one library

    Drop in a whole folder. MeetScribe transcribes any recordings it finds, spots duplicates so nothing doubles up, and builds one searchable library on your computer.

  3. Work the list

    Search by word, speaker, date or source. Or open the commitments register and see every promise across every meeting, split into what you owe and what you are waiting on.

What it does

One library for every meeting you have, in whatever shape it arrives.

Record and transcribe, offline

Record from your microphone, or give MeetScribe a recording file. It transcribes with a speech model on your own computer. The recording never leaves your machine.

Action items, with owners and due dates

“I'll send that Friday” becomes a line in a report: who, what, and the real calendar date. Across all your meetings at once, not buried in one transcript.

Decisions, on the record

Every “we decided” and “let's go with” pulled into one list, so “when did we agree to that?” has an answer.

Search across everything

One search box over every meeting in the library, with speaker, date and source filters, instead of hunting app by app.

Bulk export

The whole library, or any part of it, out to clean Markdown or text files, for your notes, your docs, or any other tool.

Private by design

MeetScribe runs locally and offline. There are no hosted accounts, and your recordings and transcripts stay with you.

Every promise, tracked: the commitments register

Pulling out action items is where other tools stop. This is where follow-through starts.

Every commitment from every meeting flows into one list, split the way you think about it: what you owe, and what you are waiting on.

The exact words, as proof

Every commitment carries the exact sentence from the transcript, with the meeting and the date one step away. When someone says “I never agreed to that,” the words are right there.

Nothing tracked without your say-so

New items wait in a confirm queue. You approve each one, fix the owner or the date, or drop it. Only you ever change a status.

Overdue and due this week

Views surface what is late and what is coming up, and undated items get their own list instead of vanishing. Due dates also export to a calendar file, so they live where you already look.

Follow-ups, already drafted

When it is time to chase, MeetScribe drafts the message, quoting the commitment, the due date, and the proof sentence. You check it and send it from your own mail. MeetScribe never sends anything itself.

Questions

Do I need to create an account?

No account. Buy it, install it, use it. MeetScribe runs on your machine, and there is nothing to sign into.

Does MeetScribe run online?

No. Recording, transcription, import, search and export all work offline, on your own computer. The only downloads are the program itself and a one-time speech model download, about 75 MB, the first time you transcribe. MeetScribe tells you the size and where it goes before it starts.

Is it a subscription?

The core tool is $29 one-time — no monthly fee. MeetScribe Pro, which adds automatic Fathom sync, is a separate optional subscription at $9/month.
