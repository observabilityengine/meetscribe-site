1. What MeetScribe is

MeetScribe is software that runs on your own computer. It records meetings or imports recordings and transcripts (including exports from Fathom, Otter, Fireflies, and Zoom), transcribes recordings offline, and extracts action items and decisions into a searchable library and a commitments register. There is no account and no hosted version of the core product.
