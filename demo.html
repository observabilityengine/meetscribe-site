What this demo can and can't do

  • The sample is the full worked example — run it as often as you like. Your own text gets one try, up to 1,500 characters. That limit is only for this page; the full product works on whole meetings, and your first 5 meetings in the app are free.
  • This demo works on transcript text, not audio. The full product transcribes recordings on your own computer — and a plain recording has no speaker labels, so who said what can be unclear there.
  • How much it catches depends on how the meeting was transcribed and how clearly people stated their commitments.
  • It reads the commitment wording in the text — things like “I'll”, “Priya will”, “assigned to” — and shows the exact sentence as proof for everything it finds.
  • Anything it finds can be checked and fixed by a person before it's trusted. Treat this as a first pass, not the final word.
